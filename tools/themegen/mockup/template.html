Cruise countdown: approach 1 vs approach 2

Both screens are 800×480, the panel's exact pixel count, and run the same effects: graded day/dusk/night, moving water, window lights that switch on and off at night, fireworks, and the glowing countdown. The difference is the source image. On the left is a real photograph, the raw material for camera projection. On the right is Disney's own CG concept render, about the best a modeled-and-rendered ship can look.

Daytime life: cloud shadows, slowly drifting clouds, gulls about every minute, the bow wave, an occasional pod of dolphins, the AquaDuck raft, and a pennant on the mast. Horn (or H) stands in for the up-down gesture. Not shown yet: the swipe moving the ship. Turn on Simulate the 18-bit panel at dusk or night to see the color banding the real panel would show in the sky and water without dithering. The finished pipeline dithers each image when it prepares it. Keys: F fireworks (night only), H horn, ← → scrub the time.
