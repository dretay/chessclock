# Disney Dream theme: sources

The Blender plates in this folder (`day.png`, `dusk.png`, `night_dark.png`, `night_lit.png`,
`mask.png`, `scene.json`) are generated by `tools/themegen/build_dream.sh` from the files
below. Those source files are kept in `assets/` at the top of the repository, which git
ignores: they're large, and the photographs are copyrighted. To rebuild on another machine,
put them back at these paths.

| File | What it is | Where it came from | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `assets/dream/disney_dream.stl` | 3D print model of the ship (124k triangles, 149 parts) | "Disney Dream" by WEDimagineer, https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:323149 | CC BY-SA (see `assets/dream/STL-LICENSE.txt`) |
| `assets/dream/side.png` | Side-profile photo of the Dream (port side, bow left), 980x500; warped onto the model as its paint | Disney Dream exterior photo, user-supplied | copyrighted, personal use |
| `assets/dream/bow.jpg` | High-resolution bow photo (`overview-disney-dream-00-full.jpg`, 2700x1080): the gold scrollwork and the anchor ornament | user-supplied (Desktop) | copyrighted, personal use |
| `assets/dream/panel_photo.jpg` | The 800x480 photo used by the mockup's photo version (panel A) | crop of a Disney Dream exterior photo, user-supplied | copyrighted, personal use |
| `assets/dream/ref/` | Reference photos used to check details (front, stern, bow). Not read by the scripts. Note: `rf10-37_41_orig1.jpg` is the Disney Wonder and `disney_fantasy_83555_orig.jpg` the Fantasy, not the Dream | the Thingiverse download's `images/` folder, and user-supplied | copyrighted |
| `assets/hdri/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky.hdr` | Daytime sky | Poly Haven, https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky | CC0 |
| `assets/hdri/qwantani_sunset_puresky.hdr` | Dusk sky | Poly Haven, https://polyhaven.com/a/qwantani_sunset_puresky | CC0 |
| `assets/hdri/*.hdr` (others) | Skies that were tried and not used | Poly Haven | CC0 |

The Poly Haven skies are the 2k `.hdr` files (`https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/HDRIs/hdr/2k/<name>_2k.hdr`, saved as `<name>.hdr`).
